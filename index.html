What is the Raw 16-Bit ADC Reading?

The ADS1115 is a high-precision external 16-bit Analog-to-Digital Converter connected to the ESP32 via I2C.

• Resolution: 16-bit (32,768 discrete integer steps)
• ADC Step Size (LSB): 0.1875 mV / count (at PGA = 6.144V FSR)
• Current Reading: 7216 counts
• Calculated Voltage: 1.353 V
• Calculation: Count × 0.0001875 V = Voltage

Why it matters: Unlike the built-in ESP32 ADC (which is only 12-bit and notoriously non-linear with high noise), the external 16-bit ADS1115 provides zero-drift, millimeter-scale head resolution directly from the pressure sensor's piezoresistive diaphragm.
